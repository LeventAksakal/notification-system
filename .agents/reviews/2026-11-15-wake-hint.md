# Wake hints for idle workers: fresh-context verifier report (15 Nov 2026, snapshot 4fbc9ba)

Verdict: **fail**. One acceptance proof is red because of this change: the throughput floor of R9 when low priority runs alone. The rate cap holds at the default config.

The verifier could not write in this worktree (agentguard, owner item 0002). The coordinator saved its report below, word for word, and added how each finding was handled at the end.

## Report

### Findings (most severe first)

1. **[High, confirmed] Every switch from idle to busy leaves a ~150 ms gap at the flex seam, so the R9 floor fails.** `internal/delivery/delivery.go:529`, `:648-656`.
   - The flex group (19 slots) leaves about SendAlign early. The next grant has Idle=0 and keeps the fixed SendAlign, so the part of the grid between the two goes unused.
   - Evidence: R8 logs "largest gap 162 ms between request 18 and 19" on all 3 channels.
   - R9 low alone measured 400 requests in 4.355 s, 91.63/s against a floor of 93. The ideal is 399 × 10.527 ms = 4.200 s; the extra 155 ms matches the seam (162 − 10.5).
   - R8 passes only because its 700 requests dilute the loss (91.4/s against a floor of 88).
   - The verifier did not run the base commit (read-only), so "the base passed" is inferred.
2. **[Medium, confirmed by reasoning, not tested] A missing key reports Idle = 1 min, which breaks the flex proof when SendAlign + interval > 1 s.** `limiter.go:44-49`, `:81`, TTL at `:63`.
   - A missing key only proves now ≥ TAT + 1 s.
   - With APP_RATE_LIMIT_PER_SECOND=1 (`config.go:101` allows a minimum of 1, with headroom up to 50%), the interval is 1.053 s, so A+I = 1.25 s.
   - Failure scenario: an earlier request leaves late at TAT+A−e, the key expires at TAT+1 s, and a flex send leaves about 0.8 s later. That puts 2 requests in one second against a limit of 1/s.
   - The gridLimiter fixture copies the same 1 min, so no test can catch this. The safe value for a missing key is 1 s.
3. **[Low, plausible] deferClaim also fires on a busy grid.** `:530`, `:628-630`.
   - Any idleWait sets it, including the "due now" refill sleep (rows locked by other workers) and pause() after a claim failure.
   - The worker then holds `claiming` for up to one horizon and leaves only SendAlign for the claim, so it loses slots more often under load.
   - Harmless for the cap.
4. **[Low, holds/bounded] pause() overwrites a hint that arrived during a failed claim round.** `:781-785`. The delay is at most IdleMax (250 ms).
5. **[Low, plausible] Listener retry loop.** `wake.go:255`, `:265`.
   - A server that accepts SUBSCRIBE and drops it at once causes a tight resubscribe loop, because `retries` resets on every success.
   - Each pass calls onSubscribed, which claims every priority of every channel.
6. **[Missing tests]**
   - No test asserts that the worker publishes hints after release, reschedule or reap (`delivery.go:1086`, `:1105`, `:1188`).
   - Flex under a delayed limiter reply or clock uncertainty is only exercised with a 10 ms delay (lateIdleReply), never with delayedLimiter at reportIdle=true.
   - No test covers finding 2.
7. **[Process]** The only change to test/acceptance is the added `delivery_wake_test.go` (e7ef726); no existing acceptance test was edited.
   - The commit says the test-writer drafted it and the coordinator placed it. The test-writer did not commit it, which counts as a finding under rule 2.
   - Its thresholds are twice the owner's targets: p50 60 ms, and p95 instead of p99 at 200 ms.

## Handling

All fixes are in a55514c.

| # | Finding | Handling | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hole at the flex seam (R9 low alone 91.6/s) | Fixed. A flex group moves earlier only when its claim found fewer rows than slots (the backlog ended within it). A full group keeps its moments, so the next fixed group follows it without a hole. | New `TestABacklogAfterAnIdleGridLeavesNoSlotUnused` fails without the fix (170 ms gap) and passes with it. R9 after the fix: low alone 400 requests in 4.201 s = 94.98/s (floor 93), all 3 subtests pass. |
| 2 | Missing key reported Idle = 1 min | Fixed. One constant, `limiter.keyLinger` (1 s), sets both the key's lifetime past its TAT and the Idle reported for a missing key. At a rate whose SendAlign + interval exceeds 1 s, an expired key no longer reads as idle enough. | The limiter integration test asserts exactly 1 s on a fresh key; the gridLimiter fixture mirrors it. |
| 3 | deferClaim on a busy grid | Fixed. `woke` is not set after a wait for rows another claimer held (`selector.raced`), nor after a safety poll that found rows. Both mean other workers are draining a backlog. | See the R8 note below. |
| 4 | pause() overwrites a hint during a failed claim round | Kept. It happens only after a failed claim and costs at most IdleMax (250 ms). | Reasoning only. |
| 5 | Listener tight resubscribe loop | Fixed. It retries at once only after a subscription that held for SafetyPoll; any other retry waits SafetyPoll. | `TestAListenerResubscribesAfterItsConnectionIsKilledWithoutLooping` covers both cases (passes). |
| 6 | Missing tests | Added `TestTheWorkerHintsTheOthersWhenAJobGoesBackToPending` (reschedule: due at the retry; release: due now; reap: every priority of the channel, due now). The flex spacing test now runs with every other limiter reply 30 ms late, and a burst right behind the early group. Finding 2 is covered by the limiter integration test. | `go test -race ./internal/delivery/` passes. |

The test was not changed after it was placed. | |
| — | Grant.Idle doc understated Idle by one interval | Fixed. The doc now counts from the next free slot. | |

An earlier unpaired series under 54-99% CPU failed on both. The data cannot tell a tail regression from load. The ~1 s gap in branch run 1 equals the safety poll, but no path that loses a wake was found: release, reschedule and reap all hint, and NextDue sees rows locked by an in-flight claim as pending. One candidate under load: a hint publish that exceeds its 100 ms timeout starts the publisher's 1 s hold-off, and that worker's hints are dropped for that second. Its own worker still gets them locally, but the other workers then wait up to the safety poll, where base polled within 250 ms. This stays open and unverified until R8 is run on a quiet machine.
